about

I recently earned a BSc in Cybersecurity at Høyskolen Kristiania, Bergen campus, and I am now pursuing an MSc in Information Security at NTNU, Gjøvik. I work across offensive security and software development. Most of my recent work starts from a concrete failure mode: AI coding assistants that reproduce vulnerable patterns from training data faster than anyone reviews them.

I compete in CTFs, mostly crypto, pwn, and web, and I volunteer as a developer at Kvarteret studenthus. My current focus is whether AI coding tools actually produce secure code, the subject of my bachelor thesis and the idea behind Riposte, an agentic security CLI for offensive and defensive work that I build with Krister Eriksen.
